Volume and staffing

Base price by guest count

The first matching tier sets the price. The base covers the minimum billable hours, and each hour past that adds the extra-hour rate. Leave the top tier's "up to" field blank so it covers any guest count.

Cart types

The multiplier applies to the base price. 1.00 means no change.

Positioning

Applied after the cart multiplier. Value and Premium also set the low and high ends of the market range.

Add-ons

A flat add-on is a fixed price per event. A per-guest add-on is multiplied by the guest count and rounded to the dollar.

Our costs used only for the margin estimate

Customers never see these. They estimate what an event costs us, so the Quote tab can show profit.
